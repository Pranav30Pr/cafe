# PROJECT_SPEC.md

Cafe website and QR table ordering system (single cafe MVP)

This file is the single source of truth for the build. Read it fully before writing any code. Work phase by phase. Do not start a phase until the previous phase's "Done when" list passes. If something is missing or unclear, ask instead of inventing content.

---

## 0. Hard rules for the agent (read first)

### 0.1 Never invent content
- Do not invent reviews, testimonials, ratings, customer counts, order counts, years in business, awards, press mentions, "since 20XX" claims, or any statistic.
- Do not invent hero text, taglines, "about us" stories, or menu descriptions. Use only text supplied by the client. Where text is missing, insert the marker `[[CLIENT_TO_PROVIDE: short description of what is needed]]`.
- Do not use superlatives or claims that cannot be proven ("best coffee in Pune", "freshest", "healthiest", "organic", "pure", "authentic") unless the client supplies them in writing with proof.
- Do not generate or use AI images. Do not use stock photos. Photos come only from the client (see section 9).
- Write plain, factual, short copy. No marketing filler.

### 0.2 Visual rules
- No purple gradients. No gradients at all.
- No pill-shaped buttons. Maximum border radius on buttons, inputs and cards is 6px.
- No emoji used as icons or decoration anywhere in the UI.
- No em dashes anywhere in user-facing copy. Use a comma, full stop, colon or the word "to" instead.
- No scroll-triggered animations, parallax, scroll-jacking, typing effects, counters that count up, or marquee text.
- No custom cursor, cursor trails or cursor-follow effects.
- No fake counters of any kind (customers served, cups sold, etc.).
- Motion is limited to simple state feedback (button pressed, panel opened) of 150ms or less, and everything must stop when `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is set.
- Colours and typography come from the client's logo and brand. Until supplied, use a neutral palette (off-white background, near-black text, one muted accent) and mark it `[[CLIENT_TO_PROVIDE: brand colours]]`.
- Icons: simple inline SVG only, always paired with a visible text label or an `aria-label`.

### 0.3 Branding and tags
- No "Made with AI", "Built with", "Powered by", "Generated by" tag, badge or footer credit from any tool, template or AI service.
- Remove `<meta name="generator">` and any similar tool tags.
- Remove all Vite and React default assets (default favicon `vite.svg`, default page title, default README text in the UI).
- An optional footer line "Website by [[CLIENT_TO_PROVIDE: developer business name]]" is allowed only if the cafe owner agrees in writing.

### 0.4 Favicon
- Build the favicon from the client's logo file (supplied in `/assets/brand/`). It must look like the logo, simplified only as needed to stay legible at 16px and 32px.
- If the logo does not shrink well, produce a simplified mark from the logo (same shape and colours) and flag it for client approval. Do not create a generic or unrelated icon.
- Deliver: `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico` (16, 32, 48), `apple-touch-icon.png` (180x180), `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, and a `site.webmanifest` that references them with the cafe's real name.
- Set `<title>` and manifest name to the real cafe name.

### 0.5 Privacy and data rules
- Collect only what is necessary (section 6.3).
- No analytics, advertising pixels, session recorders, chat widgets, or any third-party script in the MVP.
- No third-party embeds (no Google Maps iframe, no Instagram embed, no YouTube embed, no social feed widgets). Use plain links that open in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
- Self-host fonts (download open-licence fonts, for example SIL OFL, into the project). Do not load from Google Fonts or any CDN at runtime.
- No tracking cookies. Only strictly necessary storage is allowed (section 6.4).

## 1. Overview

A website for one cafe, plus a software layer that modernises how the cafe runs:

1. Public website: home, menu, about, location and hours, contact.
2. QR table ordering: each table has a unique QR code. A customer scans it, sees the menu, places an order, and the order appears on the kitchen screen with the table label. Payment happens at the shop counter, always. No online payments.
3. Kitchen screen: live incoming orders with status updates.
4. Owner dashboard: edit price and availability, view orders and basic sales summary, export customers who gave marketing consent.
5. Later phases: loyalty points, birthday and festival campaigns over WhatsApp.

### Out of scope for the MVP
- Online payments of any kind (no Razorpay, no UPI intents, no payment gateway).
- OTP verification.
- Multiple cafes (but the database must carry `cafe_id` so this can be added later).
- Delivery, takeaway, reservations.
- Customer accounts and passwords.

## 2. Tech stack

- Frontend: React + Vite + Tailwind CSS. TypeScript preferred.
- Backend: Supabase (Postgres, Auth for staff, Realtime for kitchen, Edge Functions or Postgres RPC for order creation).
- Supabase project region: Mumbai (ap-south-1) if available, otherwise the nearest Asia region.
- Hosting: Cloudflare Pages, Netlify or Vercel. Turn off any host analytics.
- No analytics SDK, no error tracking SDK that sends user data to third parties in the MVP.
- Environment variables only for the Supabase URL and anon key. Never commit a service role key.

## 3. Users and permissions

| Role | How they sign in | Can do |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | No sign in | View menu, place orders from a valid table link, view the status of their own just-placed order |
| Kitchen | Email and password (Supabase Auth), long session on the kitchen tablet | View live orders, change order status. Cannot see prices editing, sales, or the customer list |
| Owner | Email and password (Supabase Auth) | Everything kitchen can do, plus edit price and availability, view sales summary, export consented customers, manage table QR codes |
| Developer (admin) | Separate, not exposed in the UI | Edit menu structure and categories, manage staff accounts |

Role is stored in a `profiles` table keyed to `auth.users`. Row Level Security enforces roles in the database, not only in the UI.

---

## 4. Database schema (Postgres)

Use UUID primary keys. All tables have `created_at timestamptz default now()`. All tables carry `cafe_id`.

- `cafes`: id, name, legal_name, address, phone, email, fssai_number, gstin (nullable), opening_hours (jsonb), grievance_name, grievance_email, grievance_phone.
- `profiles`: user_id (fk auth.users), cafe_id, role (`kitchen` | `owner`).
- `cafe_tables`: id, cafe_id, label (for example "Table 5"), token (unique, random, at least 16 characters, URL-safe, generated with a cryptographically secure generator), is_active.
- `menu_categories`: id, cafe_id, name, sort_order, is_active.
- `menu_items`: id, cafe_id, category_id, name, description (nullable), price_paise (integer), diet (`veg` | `non_veg` | `egg`), allergen_note (nullable), is_available, image_path (nullable), image_alt (nullable), sort_order.
- `customers`: id, cafe_id, name, phone, marketing_consent (boolean default false), marketing_consent_at, consent_text_version, birthday (nullable, used only from Phase 4 and only with consent), loyalty_points (integer default 0), last_order_at, deleted_at (nullable).
- `consent_log`: id, customer_id, purpose (`order_contact` | `marketing_whatsapp`), granted (boolean), consent_text_version, created_at. Append-only. Withdrawal is a new row with `granted = false`.
- `orders`: id (uuid, used as the public order reference), cafe_id, table_id, customer_id, status (`new` | `accepted` | `preparing` | `ready` | `served` | `cancelled`), note (nullable, max 200 chars), created_at, updated_at.
- `order_items`: id, order_id, menu_item_id, item_name_snapshot, unit_price_paise_snapshot, quantity.
- `privacy_requests`: id, cafe_id, phone_or_email, request_type (`access` | `correction` | `erasure` | `withdraw_consent` | `other`), message, status, created_at, resolved_at.

Prices are stored in paise as integers. Never use floats for money.

## 5. Screens and flows

### 5.1 Public website (routes)
- `/` Home: cafe name, one line from the client, opening hours, address, link to menu. No filler sections.
- `/menu` Full menu with categories, diet marker (veg, non-veg, egg) on every item, price, allergen note where supplied, and the line "Prices are in INR [[CLIENT_TO_PROVIDE: inclusive or exclusive of taxes]]".
- `/about` Only text supplied by the client.
- `/contact` Address, phone, email, hours, plain link to the map (no iframe).
- `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/cookies`, `/order-policy`, `/accessibility` (see section 7).
- Footer on every page: legal name, address, phone, email, FSSAI licence number, GSTIN if registered, links to the five policy pages, grievance contact.

### 5.2 QR order page
- Route: `/t/:token`. The token is the only thing that identifies the table. If the token is unknown or inactive, show a plain message: "This table link is not valid. Please ask a staff member." Do not reveal which tokens exist.
- Page shows: cafe name, table label, menu with add and remove quantity controls, an order summary, and the order form.
- Order form fields: Name (required), Mobile number (required, 10 digit Indian mobile, validated), Special instructions (optional), and one unticked checkbox: "I agree to receive offers and updates from [cafe name] on WhatsApp. I am 18 or older. I can withdraw this at any time." Ordering must work with the box unticked. Never pre-tick it.
- Directly above the submit button, a short notice: why the name and number are collected (to identify and contact you about this order), with a link to `/privacy`.
- Submit button label: "Place order". After success: "Order received. Order reference: XXXX. Please pay at the counter." Show the table label.
- Each submission is a new order. There is no add-to-existing-order flow.
- The confirmation page can show live status of that one order using its UUID. It must not allow reading any other order.
- Remember name and phone for the next order only in `sessionStorage` for the current visit, and give a "Clear my details" button.
- Abuse protection: rate limit per table token and per IP (for example 5 orders per 10 minutes), maximum 30 line items per order, maximum quantity 20 per item, server-side price calculation. Do not trust prices from the client. Do not use third-party CAPTCHA in the MVP.

### 5.3 Kitchen screen
- Route: `/kitchen` (login required, role kitchen or owner).
- Live list of orders (Supabase Realtime), newest first, grouped by status.
- Each card: table label, order reference, items with quantities, note, time since placed, customer first name only (no phone number shown to kitchen).
- Buttons with clear labels: "Accept order", "Start preparing", "Mark ready", "Mark served", "Cancel order".
- New order alert: visible highlight and a short sound. Sound has a clearly labelled "Mute sound" toggle. Sound must never be the only signal.
- Must work on a tablet in landscape and on a phone.

### 5.4 Owner dashboard
- Route: `/admin` (login required, role owner).
- Menu: change price, toggle "Available" per item. Structural changes (new categories, reordering) are done by the developer.
- Orders: today's orders, count and total value by day, top items, orders by hour. Computed from real data only. Show an empty state when there is no data. Never show sample numbers.
- Tables: list tables, regenerate a table token (invalidates the old QR), download printable QR as PNG or SVG. Each QR encodes `https://[[CLIENT_TO_PROVIDE: domain]]/t/<token>`.
- Customers: list of customers with `marketing_consent = true`, export as CSV. Customers without consent are not exportable for marketing.
- Privacy requests: list and mark as resolved.

### 5.5 Order status flow
`new -> accepted -> preparing -> ready -> served`. `cancelled` is allowed from `new`, `accepted` or `preparing`. Only forward transitions plus cancel. Enforce in the database.

## 6. Security and data protection by design

### 6.1 Row Level Security
- Enable RLS on every table. Default deny.
- Anonymous users have no direct table access. They use a single RPC or Edge Function `create_order(token, items, name, phone, note, marketing_consent)` which validates the token, recomputes prices from `menu_items`, writes customer, consent_log and order in one transaction, and returns only the order reference.
- Anonymous read of a single order is allowed only by its UUID through a dedicated function that returns status and items, nothing about the customer.
- Menu tables are readable by anonymous users, limited to active and available items.
- Staff access is controlled by `profiles.role`.

### 6.2 General
- HTTPS only. HSTS enabled by the host.
- Strict Content Security Policy that allows only the site's own origin and the Supabase project origin. Since there are no third-party scripts, this must be achievable.
- Security headers: `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`, `X-Frame-Options: DENY`.
- Validate and sanitise all input on the server. Escape output. No `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`.
- Passwords handled by Supabase Auth only. Staff accounts use strong passwords.
- No phone numbers or names in URLs, logs, or error messages.
- Server logs must not store request bodies containing personal data.
- `robots.txt` and meta robots: `noindex` for `/t/*`, `/kitchen`, `/admin`.

### 6.3 Data minimisation
Collected from customers: name, mobile number, order contents, optional note, marketing consent flag with timestamp. Birthday only in Phase 4, optional, and only after marketing consent. Nothing else. No email, no address, no location, no device fingerprinting, no age or gender.

### 6.4 Browser storage and cookies
Allowed, all strictly necessary:
- Supabase Auth session for staff (`localStorage` or cookie).
- `sessionStorage` for the current cart and the temporary "remember my details" convenience.

No other cookies or storage. Cookie consent banner: not required while only the above is used and there are no analytics, ads, or third-party embeds. If anything non-essential is added later, a consent mechanism with equally prominent "Accept" and "Reject" is required before it loads, and the cookie policy must be updated first. Add a code comment and a README note stating this rule.

### 6.5 Retention (proposed, confirm with the cafe owner)
- Orders: keep 12 months, then delete or anonymise.
- Customers: keep until consent is withdrawn or erasure is requested, or 24 months after `last_order_at`, then delete.
- `consent_log`: keep as proof of consent for the period the customer record exists plus 3 years.
- Implement as a scheduled job (Supabase cron). Implement an "erase this customer" action that sets personal fields to null and removes marketing eligibility.

### 6.6 Incident handling
Create `INCIDENT_PLAN.md` with: who to call first (developer), what to preserve (logs, timestamps), how to rotate keys, and that the cafe (as Data Fiduciary) decides on notification to the Data Protection Board and affected people. Supabase backups on.

## 8. Accessibility requirements (WCAG 2.2 AA)

- Semantic HTML: one `h1` per page, logical heading order, `main`, `nav`, `header`, `footer` landmarks, a "Skip to main content" link.
- `<html lang="en">`.
- Text contrast at least 4.5:1, large text and UI component borders at least 3:1. Verify every colour pair with a contrast checker and record results in `ACCESSIBILITY_CHECKLIST.md`.
- Visible focus indicator on every interactive element, never removed.
- Full keyboard operation for the whole ordering flow, kitchen screen and dashboard. No keyboard traps. Logical tab order.
- Forms: every input has a visible `<label>`. Use `autocomplete="name"` and `autocomplete="tel"`, `inputmode="numeric"` for the phone field. Errors appear next to the field in text, linked with `aria-describedby`, and announced through an `aria-live` region. Do not rely on colour alone.
- Buttons and links have specific labels: "Place order", "Add Masala Chai to order", "Remove Masala Chai", "Mark ready for Table 5". Never "Click here", "Submit", or an icon-only button without an `aria-label`.
- Touch targets at least 44x44 CSS px.
- Status (order status, diet marker) is conveyed with text as well as colour or shape.
- Images: every meaningful image has descriptive alt text written from what is actually in the photo (supplied or confirmed by the client). Decorative images use `alt=""`. Never put "image of" in alt text. Menu photos are optional; items without a photo must still look complete.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Layout works at 200 percent zoom and on a 320px wide screen without horizontal scrolling.
- No auto-playing audio or video. Kitchen alert sound has a mute control.
- Before each phase is marked done: run Lighthouse accessibility and axe, fix all issues, and do a keyboard-only walkthrough of the flow.

## 13. Risks the developer must handle outside the code

These are not code tasks. They are flagged so they are not forgotten.
1. **Roles under the DPDP Act.** The cafe is the Data Fiduciary (it decides why customer data is collected). The developer is a Data Processor. A written agreement should say so, limit processing to the cafe's instructions, require security measures, and cover breach reporting and deletion at the end of the contract.
2. **DPDP timing.** The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 November 2025. The main duties (notice, consent, security safeguards, breach intimation, children's data, data principal rights) apply from 13 May 2027. Build to that standard now so no rework is needed.
3. **Contract capacity.** Under the Indian Contract Act, an agreement with a person under 18 is not enforceable. The service agreement with the cafe should be signed by an adult (a parent or guardian, or an adult-owned business) until the developer turns 18. Get this checked by a lawyer.
4. **Under-18 customers.** The DPDP Act restricts processing of children's data and requires verifiable parental consent in many cases. Marketing is limited to 18 and above. Ask a lawyer how ordering by minors should be handled.
5. **Mandatory phone number.** Requiring a phone number is defensible only because it is needed to identify and contact the customer about the order, and it must not be used for marketing without the separate consent. A lawyer should confirm. A lower-risk alternative is to make the phone number optional.
6. **WhatsApp.** Use only the official WhatsApp Business Platform for campaigns. Follow Meta's opt-in and template rules.
7. **FSSAI.** Display the licence number. Keep veg and non-veg markers accurate. Allergen information must be accurate or removed.
8. **Taxes and billing.** The developer's own fees may need invoices and GST registration depending on turnover. Check with an accountant. The cafe's bills still come from its own billing at the counter.
9. **No legal guarantee.** This spec reduces risk but does not make the product "lawsuit proof". A one-time lawyer review of the five policy pages and the agreement is strongly recommended.
10. **Third-party risk.** Supabase and the host are processors of the cafe's data. Keep their terms and regions documented.
